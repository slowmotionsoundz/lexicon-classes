About Me

For over 25 years, I have operated at the intersection of music production, audio engineering, and digital technology. After building three studio facilities in Huntsville, Alabama, I co-founded Slow Motion Soundz—spotlighted in Billboard Magazine as an “entertainment research and development company” and profiled in Forbes for pioneering direct-to-fan digital distribution and online monetization models via Bandcamp. Later, I expanded internationally to Landskrona, Sweden, establishing EQ Labs (EQX): a flagship recording studio, production hub, and music-tech school.

Today, I bridge this extensive foundation in audio hardware, digital music monetization, and international business with modern software engineering. I build clean, high-performance web applications with intuitive Scandinavian design, smooth animations, and robust backend architectures that solve real-world operational challenges like recurring subscription billing, cross-border European VAT compliance, and dynamic scheduling.
