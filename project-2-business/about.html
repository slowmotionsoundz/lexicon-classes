Where Scandinavian design precision meets Southern sonic character.

An Entertainment Research & Development Lab

Conceived at the turn of the millennium as an entertainment research and development lab (EQX), EQ Labs set out to solve a fundamental dilemma facing modern musicians: how to achieve commercial, label-quality sound without sacrificing independent artist ownership or creative autonomy.

Over the past 25 years, EQ Labs has grown into a unique international production collective. We bridge two legendary music traditions—the surgical acoustic precision of Scandinavia and the deep groove, soulful warmth of the American South.

This dual presence allows our engineers to offer seamless hybrid tracking, remote mixing, and reference-grade mastering to artists across Europe, North America, and around the globe.

The Dual-Hub Advantage

  • Landskrona, Sweden: European laboratory dedicated to critical listening, acoustic research, surgical mastering, and electronic sound design.
  • Huntsville, Alabama: Rocket City facility for live tracking, intimate vocal capture, vintage synthesizers, and warm analog summing.
  • Global Cloud Stem Routing: High-bandwidth digital infrastructure enabling real-time remote collaboration, review sessions, and stem mastering worldwide.

Our Studio Facilities

Flagship Acoustic Suite

Studio A • Landskrona Audio Lab

📍 Landskrona, Sweden

Designed as a surgical listening laboratory and mastering room. Featuring calibrated Scandinavian acoustic treatment, high-headroom analog summing, and pristine monitoring environments for critical mixing, final polish, and electronic sound design.

  • Calibrated Genelec active 3-way reference monitoring
  • Mastering-grade digital-to-analog converters (32-bit / 192kHz)
  • Custom acoustic diffusion & bass absorption baffling
  • High-speed transatlantic fiber link for live stem streaming
Tracking & Production Hub

Studio B • Huntsville Sound Lab

📍 Huntsville, Alabama (Rocket City)

Built for live energy, vocal intimacy, and authentic analog character. Equipped with isolated tracking booths, vintage analog synthesizers, warm tube preamps, and classic drum machine hardware to capture performances with unmatched grit and presence.

  • Isolated vocal booth with multi-mic tracking chains
  • Vintage Roland Juno-106 analog polysynth & Akai MPC sampler
