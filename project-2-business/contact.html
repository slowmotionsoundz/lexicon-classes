Session Preparation Guide

Everything you need to know before attending your session or sending stems across the Atlantic.

🎛️ Stem Preparation Guidelines

Export all stems from bar 1 beat 1 (zero start) at 24-bit or 32-bit float / 44.1kHz or higher. Leave master bus limiters and clipping off to retain dynamic headroom.

🎙️ Attended vs. Remote Sessions

Both studios support attended tracking and mixing with artist lounges. For remote clients, we broadcast real-time lossless 32-bit audio streams via transatlantic low-latency links.

⚡ Revisions & Turnaround Times

Initial mix passes are delivered in 3 to 5 business days. Every booking includes 2 rounds of stem revisions and mastered high-res WAV, MP3, and instrumental outputs.
